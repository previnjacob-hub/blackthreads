Mechanism

Intelligent by design.

01☀️

Solar Charged by Day

Integrated solar panel harvests daylight into Lithium Chemistry batteries — zero running costs, perpetual readiness. USB backup for cloudy conditions.

02🌙

Auto-Activates at Dusk

Automatically switches on at nightfall, off at dawn. No manual intervention. It watches when animals are most active — silently, every night.

03💡

360° White Flash Deterrence

Brilliant white LEDs fire randomised starburst patterns across 360°. Exploits hardwired predator aversion to sudden illumination in darkness.
